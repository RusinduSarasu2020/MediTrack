# 💊 MediTrack - Pharmacy Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

MediTrack is a web-based Pharmacy Management System designed to simplify and automate daily pharmacy operations. The system helps manage medicines, inventory, prescriptions, sales, suppliers, customers, and business analytics through a centralized platform.

The main goal of this project is to improve pharmacy efficiency, reduce manual errors, and provide a user-friendly solution for managing healthcare-related services.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Manage medicine inventory and stock levels efficiently
- Maintain accurate prescription and dispensing records
- Automate sales, billing, and payment processes
- Manage supplier and procurement activities
- Provide customer online services and order management
- Support pharmacy owners with business insights and analytics

---

## 👥 System Actors & Responsibilities

| Actor | Main Responsibility |
|------|---------------------|
| 👨‍💼 Administrator | Medicine Inventory, Stock Control & System Management |
| 👨‍⚕️ Senior Pharmacist | Prescription Verification & Dispensing Management |
| 💳 Cashier | Sales, Billing & Payment Management |
| 🚚 Supplier Coordinator | Procurement & Supplier Relationship Management |
| 👤 Customer | Customer Portal & Online Order Management |
| 📊 Pharmacy Owner | Business Analytics & Decision Support |
